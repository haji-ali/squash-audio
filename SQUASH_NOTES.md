# Squash notes

Coaching and self-notes, July 2024 to March 2026, grouped by theme. The dates show where each point came from. A point that comes up more than once is a recurring weakness.

Player: right-handed amateur, about 500 points on SportyHQ, trains weekly in a group, with occasional one-to-one coaching (James 2024, Harry 2026).

## Recurring themes (the big five)

1. **Early, high racket preparation.** This comes up in almost every session (Jul 2024, Sep 2024, Oct 2024, Feb 2026). Get the racket up as the ball leaves the front wall, before leaving the T, and move to the ball with it up.
2. **High, straight follow-through to the front wall.** Start high and finish high, with the racket pointing at the front wall. Don't cut it short (Jul 2024, Sep 2024, Oct 2024, Feb 2026).
3. **Step into the ball and transfer weight.** Get behind the ball, line the feet up with the side wall, and stomp the front foot (Oct 2024, Feb 2026, Mar 2026).
4. **Cocked, firm wrist.** Don't break the wrist to reach, don't twist it on the backhand, and don't droop the racket on the volley (Jul 2024, Sep 2024).
5. **Stay engaged at the T.** Get back fast, stand central, feet wide, knees bent, feet pointing forward, and split step. This breaks down when tired (Sep 2024, Nov 2024).

**Shot mantra (Sep 2024):** *Grip, prep, rotate, swish, follow, T.*

**Movement mantra (Nov 2024):** *STAY ENGAGED.*

## Forehand drive

- **Grip and racket face.** Open the face slightly, pointing a little upward, so it meets the ball vertically. A closed face sends the ball down (Jul 2024, Feb 2026).
- **Preparation**
  - Start with the racket at waist height, then lift it high. Keep the wrist cocked throughout (Jul 2024).
  - Lift as soon as the ball leaves the front wall (Sep 2024).
  - Don't take the racket too far behind the back. The fingers face the side wall, and the racket is high (Sep 2024).
  - Have the racket up before leaving the T (Feb 2026).
- **Swing**
  - Don't go over the top of the ball. Avoid finishing with the racket back facing the ceiling (Jul 2024).
  - Don't break the wrist to reach. Keep it cocked and use the swing and body rotation to reach (Sep 2024).
  - Use the hips and shoulders for rotation when reaching (Sep 2024).
- **Follow-through.** Go straight to the front wall and finish high. It's fine to stop it there. Using the left hand as a marker helped (Jul 2024, Sep 2024).
- **Footwork and weight transfer (Feb 2026)**
  - Step into the shot and stomp the left foot.
  - A closed stance (left foot in front) makes the weight shift easier and the shot stronger.
  - Get *behind* the ball, so the weight can travel toward the front wall.
  - Line both feet up with the side wall, so the weight moves fully from back foot to front foot.
- **Power** comes from transferring body weight while hitting, not from arm speed (Oct 2024).

## Backhand drive

- Avoid twisting the wrist, especially in preparation (Jul 2024).
- Prep: cock the racket toward the front-left corner, then swing through to the front wall (Jul 2024).
- A circular shadow motion without a ball helped groove the swing (Jul 2024).
- A slight shoulder dip once the swing is right adds power (Jul 2024).
- Have the racket up while moving to the ball (Jul 2024).
- Use the space. Don't compress yourself. Hold the racket away from the body, pointing at the back wall, which forces rotation (Feb 2026).
- **Rotation depends on where the ball is (Sep 2024):**
  - Ball at the back: move straight back next to the ball and rotate naturally.
  - Ball at the front: exaggerate the rotation.
  - Go around the ball, not at it.
- **Cross court:** very fast racket prep was the main improvement (Jul 2024).

## Volleys

- **Forehand volley**
  - Keep the wrist cocked, and don't droop the racket before lifting it (Sep 2024).
  - Racket high, above the ball and above the shoulder, then come down onto the ball (Sep 2024).
  - Shoulders face the side wall, so you hit the ball from the side, not from in front (Sep 2024).
  - Follow through toward the front wall for length (Feb 2026).
- **Backhand volley:** racket high, extend the shoulder with an intentional punch, then extend upward after contact (Sep 2024).
- **Volley-height drill (2024):** three volleys in a row aimed just above the service line, on the line, and just above the tin.

## Movement and ghosting

- **The T and readiness (Nov 2024)**
  - Get back to the T as fast as possible and stay in the centre.
  - Legs apart, knees bent, feet pointing forward.
  - Be ready to split step and push off ("a kick in the back") toward the ball.
  - Known faults: slow back to the T, drifting toward the opponent instead of the centre, feet together or turned sideways while waiting, and being too rigid. All of these get worse when tired.
  - Warm up with split steps in random directions.
- **Timing (Sep 2024):** split step as the opponent hits ("rising and stepping"). Move to the *side* of the ball. Don't chase it.
- **Back corners (Jul 2024)**
  - Move toward the service-box corner first, then go back.
  - Right corner: plant left then right foot, at 45°.
  - Left corner: plant right then left foot, at 45°.
  - Going directly back: finish open (right leg on the right, left leg on the left).
- **Middle (volley) positions (Jul 2024):** open or closed stance both work. An open-stance backhand is harder and needs more rotation.
- **Front corners (Jul 2024):** finish closed (left leg forward in the right corner, right leg forward in the left corner).
- **Ghosting technique (Sep 2024)**
  - Swish the racket through a full shot shape, from high prep to high follow-through.
  - Use speed to get back to the T.
  - Rehearse the forehand shape without a ball while walking toward the front wall.
- **Old shadow routine (about 15 min):** front middle (timing, 1-2-3), front corners, back corners, across the middle, diagonals ×2, one side (two corners) ×2, 6 points clockwise and anticlockwise, speed to box, speed to front, lightning round.

## Serve (Sep 2024)

- **Standard serve:** aim left of the mid front wall so the ball touches the side wall.
- **Lob serve:** aim higher, with the racket under the ball.
- Move back to the T as you hit.
- Drill idea: serve, then ghost.

## Tactics and match thinking

- **Two kinds of ball (self-notes)**
  - Under pressure (ball behind you, or running onto it): play a comfortable straight drive.
  - Time and an easy ball: step back, get into perfect position, step in, and play a *forcing* shot, such as a drive below the service line, a drop or a cross court. Don't waste easy balls on easy shots.
- **Separate your speed from the ball's.** On a slow ball, move fast to the right position so you can step into it.
- **Don't lean toward the back.** Picking up balls from the back feels comfortable, so stay neutral or even expect short. You have time to correct for a deep ball.
- **A drive below the service line needs rotation.**
- **Anchoring thoughts (Mar 2026).** Pick one or two thoughts to focus on instead of the score, for example "drive deep, and play a forcing shot when the ball is easy." Write them down before matches.
  - Current anchors: **step into easy shots** and **volley off the serve**, even if it costs points.

## Drills from coaching

- **Mar 2026, all from a slow feed to mid court**
  - Intentional drive: step in and hit a winning drive, then run back to the corner, boast, and reset.
  - Drop, then counter-drop.
  - Boast, then counter-drop. Take the boast low and early on the side wall, so it lands mid front wall and dies before reaching the side wall.
  - Any of the above, played to win the point.
- **2024**
  - Drive, then run to the T on the first bounce.
  - Serve, then ghost.

## Old solo routine (2024)

- Shadow movement, 15 min (as above).
- FH straight drives, 15 min: 100 shots, or 30 to the back. Throw the ball to the corner, elbow first, pushing.
- BH straight drives, 15 min: same targets. Start by bouncing the ball on the wall.
- Prep: side-to-side with a bounce (50), side-to-side volley (10), figure of eight with a bounce (50), figure of eight volley (10).
